Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Beat The Urge ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device:

  • Session history (urge ratings before/after, duration, mode)
  • Streak and progress data
  • App preferences (selected mode, duration settings)
  • Quiz answers from onboarding

This data is stored locally using AsyncStorage and is never transmitted to our servers. You can delete this data by uninstalling the app.

Analytics Data (PostHog):

We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the app experience. This includes:

  • App events (session started, session completed, feature usage)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Anonymous session identifiers

We do NOT collect:

  • Your name or email address
  • Precise location data
  • Personal identifiers
  • Content of your sessions

Purchase Data (RevenueCat):

If you make a purchase, RevenueCat processes the transaction. RevenueCat may collect:

  • Anonymous user identifier
  • Purchase history and subscription status
  • Device identifiers for purchase validation

Payment information is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the app experience
  • To track your progress and streaks locally
  • To analyze anonymous usage patterns and improve features
  • To process and manage subscriptions

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with:

  • PostHog - Anonymous analytics (hosted in EU)
  • RevenueCat - Subscription management
  • Apple - App Store purchase processing

Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app. Anonymous analytics data is retained for up to 24 months for product improvement purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app
  • Request deletion of analytics data by contacting us
  • Opt out of analytics (contact us for instructions)

Children's Privacy

Beat The Urge is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 17.

Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. All data transmissions use HTTPS encryption.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
